Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026

Somerset Animal Information Services wants Animal Information Hub UK to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology or need to adjust how content is displayed.

What we are doing

The site uses semantic headings and links where possible, visible keyboard focus styles, responsive layouts and a page-level reading control for text size, bold text and higher contrast on supported pages. You can also use browser zoom, reader settings, operating-system contrast settings and screen-reader tools.

Compliance status

This website has not yet had a full independent accessibility audit, so we cannot claim that it fully meets WCAG 2.2 AA. Some pages and features may not yet meet that standard. The site is being improved iteratively, and we intend to test navigation, contrast, keyboard access, page structure, images and documents as the Hub develops. The accessibility control is an additional aid; it does not replace accessible underlying design.

Known limitations

  • Some older pages may not yet include the same accessibility controls or navigation structure.
  • Some pages may use third-party links, externally hosted images or documents that we do not control.
  • Some image alternatives, heading structure, contrast combinations and keyboard interactions may need further checking.
  • Offline caching can mean a previously saved page is shown until the browser reconnects or refreshes its cached content.

Report an accessibility problem

If you have difficulty using any part of the site, or need information in another format, please contact us through the Hub contact page. Tell us the page URL, what you were trying to do and what would help. We will review the issue and consider a reasonable adjustment.

Enforcement and further advice

The Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) Accessibility Regulations 2018 apply to public sector bodies and some related organisations; they do not automatically apply to every private website. Equality Act 2010 duties may apply depending on the organisation and circumstances. This statement is published to be transparent about our current position, not to claim legal certification.

We plan to review this statement when major changes are made and at least annually.
