About this article

Socialisation is not about forcing an animal to meet every person or other animal. This guide will explore gradual, positive experiences that support confidence while respecting the individual animal’s comfort, health and boundaries.

Topics this guide will cover

  • What safe socialisation means
  • Gradual, positive experiences
  • Recognising stress and allowing choice
  • When to seek qualified behaviour support
