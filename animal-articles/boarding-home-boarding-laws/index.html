Boarding & Home Boarding Laws

A planned guide to dog boarding and home boarding licensing, regulations and welfare standards.

Back to Animal Articles
Article content in preparation

This page has been created as the article’s permanent home. The full article will be written and reviewed before publication; a downloadable PDF will be added in a later stage.

About this article

This guide will cover the basics of boarding dogs away from their owners, including home boarding and commercial kennels. Licensing rules and local-authority processes should be checked against current official guidance before the final article is published.

Topics this guide will cover

  • When a licence may be required
  • Welfare, accommodation and supervision standards
  • Questions owners can ask before booking
  • How to check a licence with the local authority

Important information

This page is not a substitute for professional advice. Legal requirements, licensing rules and animal welfare guidance can change. When the full article is published, check the cited official sources and seek advice from a relevant professional for individual circumstances.
