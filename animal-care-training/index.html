Animal care and training

About these guides: Information on this page is compiled and summarised from SAIS material and external sources. Where external guidance is used, we aim to link to the original source so you can check its full context and latest version. See our Sources & editorial standards. This is general information, not veterinary advice.

Practical foundations for responsible animal care: meet the animal’s needs, support learning humanely and recognise when professional advice is needed.

Key areas to consider

Use these topics as a starting point, and adapt guidance to the animal and circumstances.

01 · Guide

Welfare comes first

Consider physical health, comfort, behaviour, social needs and the animal’s ability to express normal behaviour.

02 · Guide

Training without fear

Prefer reward-based methods that teach the animal what to do. Avoid approaches that rely on intimidation, pain or frightening the animal.

03 · Guide

Safety and prevention

Plan safe handling, suitable housing, hygiene and emergency arrangements for the species in your care.

04 · Guide

Enrichment and routine

Provide appropriate opportunities to explore, forage, play, rest and make choices, tailored to the individual animal.

05 · Resource

Rescue Tips

What to look for when choosing a rescue or sanctuary, including transparency, professionalism, animal welfare and potential red flags.
