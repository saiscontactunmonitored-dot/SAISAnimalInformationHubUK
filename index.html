A sensible starting point

Care starts with understanding the animal in front of you.

Animals have different needs, and there is rarely one answer that suits every situation. Take time to learn, consider the individual animal and seek qualified help when something is not right.

Know their needsConsider health, behaviour, environment and daily care.
Check your sourcesLook for clear, relevant information and professional guidance.
Think about welfarePut the animal’s safety and wellbeing at the centre.
Ask for help earlySpeak to a vet or appropriate qualified professional when needed.
Finding support

Start with the right kind of help.

Different questions need different expertise. A directory can help you find services, while medical or urgent welfare concerns need appropriate professional support.

A

Health concerns

For illness, injury, pain or a sudden change in behaviour, contact a veterinary practice. If the problem seems urgent, seek veterinary advice straight away.

B

Care and behaviour

Look for a professional whose experience and methods suit your animal and circumstances. Ask about qualifications, approach and welfare.

C

Finding a service

Use the SAIS directory as a starting point, then check the provider’s details, suitability, credentials and current availability directly.

Our approach

Useful, clear and animal-focused.

The hub is being developed to help people navigate animal information without making it more complicated than it needs to be.

✓

Plain language

Information should be understandable and practical, without unnecessary jargon.

✓

Responsible signposting

✓

Always developing

This is the first stage of the hub. More dedicated information pages and resources can be added over time.

i

Please note

This hub is for general information and signposting. It does not replace advice from a veterinary surgeon or another appropriately qualified professional. If an animal is injured, seriously unwell or in immediate danger, contact the relevant professional service.
