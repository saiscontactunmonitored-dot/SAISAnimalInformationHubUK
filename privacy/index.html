Privacy & transparency

Privacy notice

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026

Animal Information Hub UK is an information resource powered by Somerset Animal Information Services (SAIS). This notice explains what to expect when you use this static information website.

Information we collect

The Hub is intended to be browsed without creating an account. We do not intentionally ask visitors to submit personal information through the information pages. Your browser may make standard technical requests to GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, which may process technical data such as IP address, browser details and request logs under its own arrangements.

If you contact SAIS using an external contact page, email, social network or another linked service, the information you provide is handled through that service and by the recipient for the purpose of responding. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Cookies, local storage and offline use

The Hub includes offline/PWA functionality using a service worker and browser cache to make pages available after they have loaded. The accessibility controls currently change the appearance of the page during that visit. We do not intentionally use this Hub to run advertising trackers or behavioural advertising. External websites may use their own cookies or similar technologies; check their privacy and cookie notices before using them. If analytics, embedded media or other non-essential tracking is introduced later, this notice and any required consent controls must be updated before deployment.

External links

Our pages link to animal welfare organisations, government guidance, publishers, service directories and other websites. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy practices. We cannot control their processing of your information.

How long information is kept

This Hub does not maintain a visitor account or a form-submission database as part of the static pages. Hosting providers may retain technical logs under their own retention rules. Messages sent directly to SAIS are retained only as needed to respond, keep appropriate records and meet legal obligations.

Your rights and contact

Where data protection law applies, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain uses of your personal data. These rights depend on the circumstances. To ask a privacy question, contact SAIS through the Hub contact page or the SAIS contact page.

This notice is a practical description of the current Hub, not a guarantee about the independent practices of hosting providers or external websites. It should be reviewed whenever the site adds forms, analytics, advertising, embedded services or other data collection.
