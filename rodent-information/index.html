Small mammals and rodents

Small mammals have different needs despite their size. Choose guidance for the exact species rather than treating all small pets alike.

Browse animal categories
Practical information · Welfare first

Key areas to consider

Use these topics as a starting point, and adapt guidance to the species and individual animal.

01 · Guide

Identify the species

Rats, mice, hamsters, guinea pigs and other small mammals differ in diet, social needs, handling and housing. Use species-specific advice.

02 · Guide

Housing and enrichment

Provide a secure, appropriately sized environment with hiding places, suitable bedding and activities that allow natural behaviours.

03 · Guide

Food and water

Use a species-appropriate diet and provide fresh water. Avoid sudden changes and check that each animal can access food and water.

04 · Guide

Handling and observation

Approach calmly and learn safe handling for the species. Watch for changes in appetite, breathing, movement, droppings or normal activity.

Important information

Small animals may hide signs of illness and can worsen quickly. Seek advice from a vet experienced with the species if you notice concerning changes.
