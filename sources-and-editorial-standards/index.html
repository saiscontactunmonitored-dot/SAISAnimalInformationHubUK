Trust & transparency

Sources & editorial standards

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026

Animal Information Hub UK is powered by Somerset Animal Information Services (SAIS). It is an independent information resource intended to help readers find useful animal-care guidance and reputable organisations. It is not a veterinary practice, regulator or substitute for professional advice.

Where information comes from

Pages may be informed by public guidance from government departments, animal welfare charities, veterinary organisations, universities, specialist organisations, books and other published resources. Where a page uses an external source, we aim to link to it near the relevant information or in a sources section. A link means the reader can consult the original publication and check its context and latest version.

Some content is written or summarised by SAIS using multiple sources; it is not necessarily copied from any single website. A source link does not mean that the organisation endorses this Hub or has reviewed our wording. We aim to distinguish our own summaries from quoted or reproduced material.

Copyright, quotations and images

Text, photographs, illustrations, charts and other creative works can be protected by copyright even when they are publicly viewable online. We aim to use original material, properly licensed material, or material used with permission, and to give clear attribution where required. A credit or hyperlink alone does not automatically grant permission to reproduce someone else’s work. If you believe content on this site infringes your rights, please contact SAIS using the contact page and include the page URL, the material concerned and evidence of your rights so it can be reviewed.

Checking and updating

Animal welfare guidance, legislation, products and veterinary recommendations can change. We aim to prioritise authoritative and current sources, check important claims, and update pages when we become aware of material changes. However, not every page has been independently peer-reviewed, and we cannot guarantee that every item is complete, current or suitable for every animal or situation. Dates and sources should be checked before relying on information in a time-critical situation.

Animal health, welfare and emergencies

Information on this Hub is general educational guidance only. It is not a diagnosis, prescription, or individual care plan. Contact a veterinary surgeon for animal health concerns. If an animal may be seriously ill, injured, poisoned, overheated or in immediate danger, seek urgent veterinary or relevant professional assistance rather than waiting for a website response.

Corrections and source requests

If you spot a factual error, outdated link, missing attribution or accessibility issue, please tell us via the contact page. Include the page address, the specific issue and a reliable source where possible. We will review reports, but cannot promise a particular response time.

Important note about external links

External links are provided to help readers find further information. SAIS does not control third-party websites, their content, availability, privacy practices or commercial recommendations. Inclusion of a link is not an endorsement unless we explicitly say so.
